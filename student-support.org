* Student Support

JRC 2027 strongly encourages the participation of students and early-career
researchers in statistics, quality, industry, and technology. The following
opportunities are available.

** Mary G. and Joseph Natrella Scholarship

The *Mary G. and Joseph Natrella Scholarship* is awarded annually by the
Quality and Productivity (Q&P) Section of the American Statistical
Association (ASA) to support graduate students pursuing degrees with a
demonstrated interest in the application of statistics to quality and
productivity.

The scholarship honors the careers of Mary G. Natrella (author of the
classic NBS/NIST handbook /Experimental Statistics/) and her husband,
Joseph Natrella.

*** Award Package

Recipients of the scholarship typically receive:
- A *$3,500* research grant
- A *$500* travel stipend
- *Complimentary registration* for the entire conference and its pre-conference
  short course
- An opportunity to *present their research* at JRC 2027

*** Eligibility & Application

- Open to full-time graduate students (master's or doctoral level)
- Evaluated on academic scholarship, teaching and mentoring, leadership and
  service in the statistics community, and demonstrated experience in
  statistical applications
- *Application Deadline:* TBA
- For full background and past winners, visit the
  [[https://community.amstat.org/qp/scholarshipsawards/marygandjosephnatrellascholarship][ASA Q&P Natrella Scholarship]] page.

** Student Paper Competition :noexport:

JRC 2027 will hold a student paper competition. Graduate students are
invited to submit papers on methodology, applications, or case studies
relevant to the conference themes.

- Full papers (rather than just short abstracts) will be required for
  consideration.
- Competition winners will receive awards, travel support, and dedicated
  presentation slots at the conference.
- Detailed eligibility criteria, formatting guidelines, and submission
  instructions will be announced when submissions open.

** Call for Papers & Contributed Submissions :noexport:

In addition to student-specific competitions, all students, academics, and
industry practitioners are invited to submit abstracts for *contributed talks*
(20-minute presentations) and *poster presentations*.

*** Topics of Interest

Submissions are welcome on all areas of statistics with applications to
quality, industry, and technology, including:

- Statistical quality control and process monitoring
- Design of experiments (classical, computer, and adaptive designs)
- Response surface methodology
- Reliability, lifetime data analysis, and survival analysis
- Industrial statistics and engineering applications
- Machine learning and AI for quality and manufacturing systems
- Big data analytics and high-dimensional inference
- Spatial and environmental statistics
- Bayesian methods in quality and reliability
- Measurement systems analysis and gauge studies
- Computer experiments and surrogate modeling

*** Submission Guidelines

- Submissions will consist of a title and a short abstract (approximately
  200–300 words).
- Authors may submit for a *contributed talk*, *poster*, or both.
- Proposals for *invited sessions* (3–4 speakers on a unified topic) are
  also welcome and should be sent directly to the Program Chairs.
