* Short Courses

JRC 2027 will offer one or more *pre-conference short courses* on Monday, June 21, 2027 — the day before the main conference begins.

Short courses are full-day (or half-day) workshops designed to provide hands-on, in-depth training on cutting-edge topics in statistics, data science, and related fields. They are open to all conference registrants as well as non-attendees (subject to capacity).

** 2027 Short Course(s)

/Short course details — title, instructor, and description — will be announced soon./

** Format

- *Date:* Monday, June 21, 2027
- *Location:* University of Connecticut, Storrs (specific room TBA)
- *Duration:* Full day (approximately 9:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.)
- *Includes:* Coffee breaks and lunch

** Registration and Fees

Short course registration is separate from main conference registration. Fees and registration details will be announced when the course is confirmed.

Discounted rates may be available for ASA members and students.
