** What Is Included

Conference registration is expected to include:

- Attendance at all technical sessions, keynote, and plenary talks
- Breakfasts, lunches, and coffee breaks on conference days
- Conference banquet
- UConn Tech Park tour

Short course registration is expected to include coffee breaks and lunch on June 21, 2027.

** Student Support

Limited travel support awards may be available for student participants. Details
will be posted when confirmed.

Students are encouraged to participate in the student paper competition — see the [[../call-for-papers/][Call for Papers]] page.
