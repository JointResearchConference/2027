* Call for Papers

JRC 2027 invites submissions of abstracts for *contributed talks* and *poster presentations* on research and applications at the intersection of statistics, quality, industry, and technology.

The conference will also consider proposals for *organized invited sessions*. Researchers interested in organizing a session of 3–4 speakers on a focused topic are encouraged to contact the Program Chairs.

** Topics of Interest

Submissions are welcome on all topics in statistics with applications to quality, industry, and technology, including but not limited to:

- Statistical quality control and process monitoring
- Design of experiments (classical, computer, and adaptive designs)
- Response surface methodology
- Reliability, lifetime data analysis, and survival analysis
- Industrial statistics and engineering applications
- Machine learning and AI for quality systems
- Big data analytics and high-dimensional inference
- Spatial and environmental statistics
- Bayesian methods in quality and reliability
- Functional data analysis
- Statistical process control and improvement
- Computer experiments and surrogate modeling
- Measurement systems analysis and gauge studies
- Robust parameter design and engineering optimization

** Submission Guidelines

- Submissions should consist of a *title* and a short *abstract* (approximately 200–300 words)
- Authors may submit as a *contributed talk* (20-minute presentation), *poster*, or both
- Invited session proposals should include a session title, brief description, and names and affiliations of proposed speakers (3–4) and the session organizer
- Submission details and the online submission portal will be announced when the call opens

** Student Paper Competition

JRC 2027 will hold a student paper competition. Full papers (rather than just abstracts) will be required for competition entries. Details on eligibility, submission format, and deadlines will be announced separately.

Prizes and travel support awards may be available for competition winners.
