* Venue & Accommodation

** Conference Venue

JRC 2027 will be held at the *University of Connecticut, Storrs* campus.

The University of Connecticut is a flagship public research university and home
to a highly-regarded Department of Statistics. The Storrs campus is located in
northeastern Connecticut, approximately:

- 45 miles from Hartford, CT (Bradley International Airport — BDL)
- 90 miles from Boston, MA
- 100 miles from New York City, NY

Specific venue buildings and room assignments will be posted closer to the conference date.

*** UConn Tech Park

JRC 2027 will include tours of the *UConn Tech Park*, a state-of-the-art research and innovation facility featuring:

- Advanced imaging and materials characterization labs
- Manufacturing innovation center
- Energy technology labs
- Industry partnership facilities

*** Getting to UConn Storrs

*By air:*
- *Bradley International Airport (BDL)* — Hartford/Springfield, CT (~45 min drive)
- *T.F. Green Airport (PVD)* — Providence, RI (~1 hr drive)
- *Logan International Airport (BOS)* — Boston, MA (~1.5 hr drive)

*By car:* UConn Storrs is accessible from I-84 via Route 195 or Route 44.

*By bus:* Peter Pan Bus Lines and CT Transit provide service to Storrs from Hartford, and connecting service from other major cities.

*** Parking

Visitor parking is available on the UConn Storrs campus. Details on parking permits and locations for conference attendees will be posted closer to the event.
